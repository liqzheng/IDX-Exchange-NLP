Week 4 accuracy report: QueryParser on Week 1 sample queries

Data: 91 queries from data/processed/sample_queries.json
Full output: reports/week4_parse_results.txt

Method
- Manual review of every parsed result.
- A query counts as correct if every constraint the database can express
  (price, bedrooms, bathrooms, city, pool/fireplace/view, negation) was
  extracted correctly and nothing extra was extracted.
- Constraints with no matching column (HOA, seller financing, open house,
  closing date, schools) are out of scope and not counted.

Result
- First run: about 79 of 91 correct (87%).
- After fixes: 89 of 91 correct (98%).
- Fixes made after reading the first run: plural "pools", county names
  (Orange County) and state (California) wrongly read as city, decimal and
  word prices ("1.2m", "1 million"), "pre-approved for 850k".

Known gaps
- Two queries compare two cities (Irvine vs Anaheim); only one city is kept.
- data/schema.json was built from the 1000-listing sample, so real cities
  such as Sacramento are flagged as "not found". It should be built from
  the full database.
- No amenity columns exist for solar, RV parking, gym, home office.

Limitations
- The fixes were made while looking at these same 91 queries, so 98% is
  optimistic and not a held-out score.
- Review was done by reading outputs, not against a separately written
  gold file.
- Tests: 196 pytest cases pass, including 5 SQL injection cases and
  schema validation cases.tail -4 reports/week4_accuracy.txt
